THE BEGINNING

Stephan and Aron first knew each other in sixth grade. After Aron moved away, they met again at Glemmen VGS. A shared first year in IT and Media Production became a second year focused on Media Production, and filmmaking became the thing that kept bringing them back together.

Today Stephan works with video and podcast production as an apprentice content producer at Studio Wallin, while Aron works as a content producer at BAMA. STAF Films is where that professional experience meets independent narrative filmmaking.

Make ambitious films, be honest about the process, and keep climbing from one project to the next.
