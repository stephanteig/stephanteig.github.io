ABOUT / PROFILE
DIRECTOR · CO-WRITER · PRODUCER · ACTOR

Aron Feren.

A Norwegian filmmaker, actor and content producer, drawn to horror, cosmic uncertainty and films that leave the audience questioning what they have just seen.

Aron’s connection to performance began through acting and theatre, including an early screen appearance in the documentary Mannen fra Snåsa. Today he works as a content producer for BAMA while developing independent projects through STAF Films. Earlier screen credits are listed under his former name, Aron Benedict Magnus Folkestad.
