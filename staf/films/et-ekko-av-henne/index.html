Et ekko
av henne.

▶ WATCH TRAILER ↗ IMDB ↗ LETTERBOXD ↗

Some voices never fade, even from beyond.

Years after the tragic disappearance of his teenage daughter, a grieving father begins receiving voicemails from her phone. At first they seem like echoes from the past. When the messages start referring to the present, he is pulled into a desperate search through hidden secrets, unresolved guilt and a mystery that may be more than coincidence.

Is it a cruel deception, or a final gift from the other side?
