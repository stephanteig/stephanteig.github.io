ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

Short films made by students in film and media production at Glemmen, shown on a real cinema screen.

CUT! ran twice on 10 February: a daytime screening for school classes and an evening screening open to everyone. Et ekko av henne was the headliner, followed by a panel talk with its director and producer about film, the festival and working in media. Stephan and Aron helped create and run it: planning, submissions, coordination and the practical delivery of a cinema event. An event, not a STAF film.
